Our Food, Our Passion

Welcome to Desi Dastarkhawan!

Desi Dastarkhawan celebrates the rich and authentic taste of Pakistani home cooking. Our idea is simple: serve familiar desi dishes with care, warmth and the flavours people love.

From aromatic biryani to comforting daal chawal, our menu is inspired by traditional recipes and the joy of sharing food with family and friends.
